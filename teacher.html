Getting started

  1. Make your students' accounts. Under Students: choose how many, and the game makes a code and a password for each.
  2. Print the login slips and hand one to each student. Write each student's name on their slip. The game never stores names.
  3. Students log in and play. They type the code on their slip (for example rizal-07) and the password. Their progress shows here.

Test Questions and Answers

Edit each test's questions and correct answers, and the trivia card shown before the pre-test. Tick the circle beside the correct answer.

Edit before a session, not during one: changing questions after students have answered makes the pre-test and post-test harder to compare. Pre-test and post-test questions should be matched pairs: the same topic and difficulty, in different words.

Talaan Papers

Up to three papers students find on the road and keep in the Talaan. Where each one lies is fixed by the game; you write what it says.

Students see the papers in Tagalog or English, exactly as you type them. A paper that gives away a post-test answer word for word turns the post-test into a reading check.
